## Proposed email

Subject — Could I test a small textile catalogue tool with your team?

Hi team,

I’m an AI solutions architect testing a small prototype that extracts colour and material from supplier descriptions and flags missing or ambiguous source information before quoting for processing.

Would your team be open to a free test on up to 20 home-textile products? A CSV containing product IDs and descriptions is enough. Please remove customer details, prices and confidential commercial information. I would return a draft attribute file, source quotations and a report of missing information, then ask for a short review against your usual preparation process.

The prototype is limited to seven colours and four materials. I’m trying to establish whether it saves an operator time. Rules and AI produced the same number of delivered rows in our small external-data diagnostic, so I would value a candid comparison with your current workflow.

If useful, I can send the demo and sample format first. Any use of your name, data or feedback in public materials would require a separate agreement.

Best,
Maciej Rubczyński

## Pilot procedure

1. Ask for an initial call or permission to send the demo before requesting data.
2. Agree which non-confidential fields can be used and where results can be retained.
3. Use up to 20 descriptions, with no customer or commercial data. Import IDs and descriptive names into the app schema, documenting any placeholder price fields required by the prototype.
4. Have the reviewer establish expected values and prepare the same batch using their normal method.
5. Compare preparation plus review time, incorrect acceptances, missing-field detection and usefulness of the delivered CSV.
6. Treat a 30% time reduction at equal or better quality and interest in a repeat batch as a proposed continuation threshold, rather than an established result.
